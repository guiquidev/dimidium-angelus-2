Features
- turn-based combat
- progression through rng rooms
- two currencies - gold and evil thingy

Rooms
- combat
- shop
- heal/bonfire
- events (?)

Combat
- attacks, skills and buffs
- items with various effects
- energy based, like in card games
- player actions:
	laedo - basic attack
	impetus - do damage, take damage
	vulnero - reduce armor
	contego - shield
	devoveo - take damage, gain mana
